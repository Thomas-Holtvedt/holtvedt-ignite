# The Death of Datafunn
Me and my close friend Chris started Datafunn back in 2023, with the mission to democratize data. Getting publicly "available" data and making it truly available through visualization and proper data handling. While we still believe in the mission and the potential, life came in the way and made us prioritize differently, it therefore made the most sense to shut down the company. Even though the company failed, I would not call it a failure, but in fact quite the opposite. 

While we never achieved the commercial success we had hoped for, Datafunn have brought me a lot of fun, new skills and lessons I will carry with me for the rest of my life

## Why Shutting Down is the right choice
So why shut it down and not just keep it running? The costs are low and one day we might've started up again.
Well, I firmly believe that one should do it properly or not at all, and with all the other side projects I have going on it feels better to focus properly on something than a sliver of attention to everything.
