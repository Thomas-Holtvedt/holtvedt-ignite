# Find your language
When I initially embarked on the quest to create my own website, I set out with a clear goal in mind "Build it the old school way".
That meant that I was not to use any frameworks, or bundlers or anything like that. Only HTML, CSS and JS if needed, the reason is that I wanted to learn basics and also simultaneously challenge and simplify my journey.
I got my site 90% there, everything in place exactly how I wanted it except one core feature: The blog.


## YouTube
I decided to document the process of creating my basic website on YouTube, a kind of a "Web Dev Log".

Although I enjoyed the challenge and the result, I hated the languages. HTML, CSS and JS, I don't like any of them and the thought of writing my blog content in them gives me nightmares, so it was time to look into options.
This time I went in with another goal: "Happiness". These projects takes time and effort, so my own happiness while working with them is obviously paramount. The language that I choose would have to bring me happiness and joy, and going through my options I was thinking of this one language I have been flirting with for a while, without ever daring to take the step: Swift.
For someone who at the time had never even owned a mac, the choice may seem weird, but there's something about it that called to me, and I have on several occasions stolen my wife's mac and played around with it. And also it gets more and more accessible on Windows and Linux as well.
